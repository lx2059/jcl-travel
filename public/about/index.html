About JCL Travel

Opening the Door to China for Our Overseas Colleagues

Our Story

At JCL Travel, we believe travel is about connection, discovery, and transformation.

Founded with a passion for sharing the real China with the world, we noticed that many international travelers were missing out on authentic Chinese experiences, often limited to crowded tourist sites and standardized tours.

We set out to change that by creating tour packages that go beyond the surface, connecting travelers with local communities, traditions, and landscapes that make China truly special.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide international travelers with authentic, meaningful, and unforgettable experiences that showcase the richness of Chinese culture, history, and natural beauty while fostering cross-cultural understanding and respect.

Our Values

🏮

Authenticity

We show the real China through genuine interactions with local people and off-the-beaten-path destinations.

⭐

Quality

Highest standards in guides, accommodations, and transportation for your comfort and satisfaction.

🌱

Sustainability

Responsible tourism that benefits local communities and preserves China's heritage.

✏️

Personalization

Tours tailored to your unique interests, preferences, and travel style.
