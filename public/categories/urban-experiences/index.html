Shanghai Modern Metropolis Tour

Tour Overview

Discover Shanghai, China’s most dynamic city where East meets West in spectacular fashion. This 5-day tour takes you from ancient water towns to futuristic skyscrapers, showcasing the incredible diversity of this global metropolis.

Perfect for first-time visitors and repeat travelers alike, our Shanghai tour offers a balanced mix of must-see attractions and authentic local experiences.

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Shanghai

  • Airport pickup and transfer to your hotel in central Shanghai
  • Welcome briefing with your guide over traditional Chinese tea
  • Evening at leisure to recover from travel or explore your neighborhood
  • Accommodation: 4-star hotel in People’s Square area

Day 2: Historic Shanghai & The Bund

  • Morning: Visit Yu Garden and the adjacent Old City God Temple area
  • Lunch: Authentic Shanghai soup dumplings (Xiaolongbao) at a local restaurant
  • Afternoon: Explore the historic Bund waterfront with its colonial architecture
  • Evening: Huangpu River cruise with stunning views of Pudong’s skyline
  • Dinner: Welcome dinner featuring Shanghai cuisine

Day 3: French Concession & Modern Art

  • Morning: Walking tour through the charming French Concession
  • Visit: Tianzifang arts and crafts enclave
  • Lunch: Fusion cuisine in Xintiandi
  • Afternoon: Shanghai Museum (private guided tour)
  • Evening: Optional acrobatics show or free time for shopping

Day 4: Pudong & Future Shanghai

  • Morning: Visit the Shanghai Tower observation deck (632 meters)
  • Experience: Traditional Chinese tea ceremony
  • Lunch: Modern Chinese cuisine with Pudong skyline views
  • Afternoon: Shanghai Urban Planning Exhibition Center
  • Evening: Farewell dinner at a revolving restaurant

Day 5: Departure or Extension

  • Morning: Visit Zhujiajiao water town (optional)
  • Afternoon: Transfer to airport for departure
  • Extension options: Add days for Suzhou, Hangzhou, or Beijing

Tour Highlights

🏛️ Cultural Immersion

  • Private guided tour of Shanghai Museum’s best collections
  • Traditional tea ceremony with tea master
  • Walking tour through historic neighborhoods

🏙️ Architectural Wonders

  • The Bund’s colonial buildings contrasted with Pudong’s futuristic skyline
  • French Concession’s tree-lined avenues and art deco buildings
  • Shanghai Tower - China’s tallest building

🍜 Culinary Experiences

  • Authentic Shanghai soup dumplings cooking demonstration
  • Huangpu River cruise dinner
  • Local market visit and food tasting

🛍️ Shopping Opportunities

  • Nanjing Road - China’s premier shopping street
  • Tianzifang arts and crafts district
  • Xintiandi’s boutique shops

Accommodation

Standard Package

4-star hotel in central Shanghai (People’s Square or Nanjing Road area)
