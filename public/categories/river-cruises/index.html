River Cruises

Chongqing & Yangtze River Gateway

Tour Overview

Discover Chongqing, China’s most unique metropolis built on hills at the confluence of two rivers. This 5-day tour combines urban exploration with a Yangtze River cruise through the breathtaking Three Gorges.

Quick Facts

  • Duration: 5 days / 4 nights
  • Price: From $1,350 per person
  • Difficulty: Moderate (hilly terrain)
  • Best time: March-November
  • Group size: 2-12 travelers

Itinerary Highlights

Day 1: Arrival in Mountain City

  • Airport transfer to Chongqing
  • Visit Hongya Cave for night views
  • Authentic Chongqing hot pot dinner

Day 2: Urban Exploration

  • Ciqikou Ancient Town
  • Chongqing People’s Assembly Hall
  • Three Gorges Museum
  • Yangtze River cable car ride

Day 3: Dazu Rock Carvings

  • Day trip to UNESCO World Heritage Dazu Rock Carvings
  • Lunch featuring local Sichuan cuisine
  • Return to Chongqing for evening at leisure

Day 4: Yangtze River Cruise

  • Transfer to cruise ship
  • Begin Yangtze River cruise
  • Shore excursion to Fengdu Ghost City
  • Captain’s welcome dinner

Day 5: Three Gorges & Departure

  • Cruise through Qutang, Wu, and Xiling Gorges
  • Optional shore excursion to Shennong Stream
  • Disembark and transfer to airport
