Beijing Imperial Capital Tour

Tour Overview

Experience the grandeur of China’s imperial past in Beijing, the capital for over 800 years. This 6-day tour combines must-see historical sites with authentic cultural experiences, culminating in a visit to the magnificent Great Wall.

Quick Facts

  • Duration: 6 days / 5 nights
  • Price: From $1,450 per person
  • Difficulty: Moderate (some walking and stairs)
  • Best time: April-May, September-October
  • Group size: 2-12 travelers

Itinerary Highlights

Day 1: Arrival & Temple of Heaven

  • Airport transfer and hotel check-in
  • Visit Temple of Heaven Park
  • Watch locals practicing tai chi and other exercises
  • Welcome dinner featuring Beijing cuisine

Day 2: Forbidden City & Tiananmen Square

  • Morning tour of Tiananmen Square
  • Private guided tour of the Forbidden City
  • Jingshan Park for panoramic views
  • Evening Peking Duck banquet

Day 3: Great Wall Adventure

  • Full-day excursion to Mutianyu Great Wall
  • Cable car ride up, optional toboggan down
  • Lunch at local restaurant near the Wall
  • Return to Beijing for evening at leisure

Day 4: Summer Palace & Old Beijing

  • Boat ride on Kunming Lake at Summer Palace
  • Explore Hutongs (traditional alleyways) by rickshaw
  • Visit local family for tea in their courtyard home
  • Optional Kung Fu show in the evening

Day 5: Lama Temple & Art District

  • Visit Yonghe Lama Temple (Tibetan Buddhist temple)
  • Explore 798 Art District (contemporary art galleries)
  • Free afternoon for shopping or additional sightseeing
  • Farewell dinner

Day 6: Departure

  • Visit Panjiayuan Antique Market (weekends only)
  • Transfer to airport for departure
